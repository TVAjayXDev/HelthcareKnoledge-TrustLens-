The 5-Tier Authority Ladder

When policies intersect, TrustLens applies this strict linear precedence ladder to eliminate conflicting clinical directions.

1 Regulatory Statutes
Supreme Authority

Federal & State Laws, CMS Guidelines, DEA Scheduling, State Department of Health codes. Cannot be overridden under any condition.

2 Clinical Guidelines
National Consensus

NCCN, ACC/AHA, CDC, Joint Commission Evidence Protocols. Gold-standard professional society directives.

3 Payer Rules
Binding Reimbursement

Commercial & Medicare Payer Prior-Auth criteria, formulary step therapies, and coverage determination guidelines.

4 Institutional SOPs
Northfield Health Ratified

Northfield Health Standard Operating Procedures, pharmacy formularies, hospital infection control manuals, and nursing bylaws.

5 Department Memos
Informational Only

Floor memos, weekly rotation circulars, clinical shift handoffs. Explicitly superseded by all higher tiers.

If two authoritative hospital documents provide divergent dosage ranges or protocols, the system flags the contradiction immediately rather than hallucinating an average.

Outdated documents (>365d without re-ratification) and unapproved floor drafts are strictly quarantined from runtime inference generation.
