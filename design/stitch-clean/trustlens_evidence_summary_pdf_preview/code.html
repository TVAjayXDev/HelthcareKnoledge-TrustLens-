Under Emergency Department Standing Protocol 4.2, registered nurses are authorized to initiate potassium chloride IV replacement (rates ≤20 mEq/hr) without prior physician co-signature for adult patients presenting with confirmed serum K⁺ < 3.2 mEq/L [1]. However, pre-infusion renal clearance must be validated with a serum creatinine <1.8 mg/dL obtained within the preceding 4-hour window [2]. If transferring out of the ED to general floor care, or in cases involving pediatric cohorts (<18 years), direct attending physician countersignature is strictly mandatory prior to infusion bag hang [3].
